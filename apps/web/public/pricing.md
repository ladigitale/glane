# Pricing

Glane is **free**. There is no paid plan, no tier, no subscription and no in-app purchase.

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|------|-------|--------------|
| Free (only plan) | 0 € — no cost | The full instrument: capture, library, synth, 6-track sequencer, exports (WAV, MP3, Octatrack, clip), unlisted listen links, Claude connector (MCP) |

- **Hosted app**: <https://glane.tadaaa.space> — free to use, no account needed for local work. An account (username + password) is only needed to publish listen links or link SoundCloud.
- **Self-hosting**: the source code is open source under the MIT license — <https://github.com/ladigitale/glane>. Running your own instance costs only your own hosting.
- **Your data**: audio stays on your device (local-first); no storage plan to buy.

Third-party services you choose to connect (Claude, SoundCloud, Bandcamp) have their own pricing.
