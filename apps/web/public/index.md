# Glane

Glane is a web instrument for capturing, segmenting and arranging ambient sounds: **capture → segment → library → arrange**. It runs in the browser (installable PWA) and is local-first: audio stays on your device until you choose to export or publish a listen link.

## What you can do

- **Capture** — record from the microphone or import a file; Glane isolates interesting fragments and stores them locally.
- **Library** — browse, filter by session, class or tag, preview and compare your sounds (on-device analysis: tags, pitch, loudness, loops).
- **Synth** — generate textures and motifs from your references; they land in the same library as live captures.
- **Arrange** — a 6-track sequencer with FX; export WAV, MP3, Octatrack slices or a vertical clip, or share an unlisted listen link.

## Who it is for

Field recordists, sound designers and musicians who capture audio outdoors or in uncontrolled spaces, then segment, tag and rearrange material on a laptop or tablet.
