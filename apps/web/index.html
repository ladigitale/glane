Glane — field-recording instrument

Capture the field, glean the sounds, arrange — then share a listen. Glane is a local-first web instrument: record or import ambient sound, segment it into a sample library with on-device analysis, generate synthetic textures, and arrange everything on a 6-track sequencer. Audio stays on your device until you choose to export or publish a listen link.

Free and open source (MIT). Glane needs JavaScript to run.

Privacy

By default audio stays on this device. Segments detected as speech are excluded from the library and are not uploaded. The recording indicator always stays visible. Full privacy notes.
